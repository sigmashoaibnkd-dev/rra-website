About Us

Turning voices into meaningful action.

Rohingya Rise Alliance is a community-driven initiative created to connect people, amplify voices and encourage positive change.

We create space for people to share their experiences, creativity, knowledge and ideas. Our community welcomes participation from people around the world who believe in dignity, peace, equality and opportunity.
